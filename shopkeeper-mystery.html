PROJECT 05 / SHORT FICTION

THE SHOPKEEPER MYSTERY

Four bodies, one missing person, and an old restaurant owner's rule: after eight, nobody uses real names.

FIRST-PERSON NARRATIVEMYSTERY / SUSPENSESHORT FICTION
AFTER 8:00 P.M. / USE NUMBERS, NOT NAMES

Story overview

The fourth body is found at 6:40 in the morning. The narrator and a partner arrive in a small town where four people have died under unexplained circumstances — and a fifth person is missing. An old waiter points them toward a restaurant owned by Rao, a retired police investigator who follows a strange rule: after eight at night, employees use numbers instead of real names.

Rao explains that two of his late-shift employees died after being approached by someone who knew their real names, even though the person had never been introduced. He admits that he has no evidence linking the pattern together; after thirty years investigating murders, all he has is a recurring detail that frightens him enough to change how his restaurant operates.

The narrator stays to investigate. At 11:47 p.m., an unknown number calls. The line stays open in silence, then a voice whispers the narrator's real name — a name that nobody in the town should know. Across the road, Rao stares directly at the narrator, locks his front door, and turns off the lights. The narrator finally understands the reason for the rule.

“After eight, we don't use real names.”

How the suspense builds

  • A pattern before an explanation: the four deaths and one disappearance give the story a concrete question.
  • A rule that sounds absurd: Rao's policy encourages the reader to doubt him while also raising the stakes.
  • Escalating time pressure: the story tracks the hours toward the moment the rule becomes active.
  • A personal final threat: the unknown caller says the narrator's real name, turning a reported pattern into immediate danger.

Form and tone

The piece is told in first person, holding the reader close to the narrator's limited understanding. Information arrives through interviews, quiet behavioural details, and timing. The explanation remains incomplete at the end, leaving the story with unease rather than resolving the phenomenon.
