PROJECT 06 / GAME CONCEPT + SYSTEMS DESIGN

RECON

A multiplayer strategy, management, and combat game about building a mafia organisation, coordinating specialised departments, and competing against rival gangs. The police operate as an NPC threat, not a playable faction.

Concept overview

RECON is a multiplayer strategy, management, and combat game in which players build and operate a shared mafia organisation. Players manage specialised departments, NPC workforces, facilities, logistics, finances, and security. Rival gang warfare takes place through matchmaking and attack operations against an organisation's entire base; the game is not a persistent shared open world.

Organisation and five starting roles

An organisation begins with five player positions. One HQ Boss leads the organisation, supported by four Department Bosses. The HQ Boss oversees the whole organisation, approves attacks, manages organisation-wide finances, purchases expansion properties, and operates central surveillance.

01

Leads the organisation, manages HQ finances and legitimate businesses, appoints Department Bosses, approves attack requests, and oversees central surveillance.

Manages production facilities, raw materials, equipment, NPC workers, production workflows, and finished-product storage.

Manages packaging materials, stations, NPC workers, workflows, and finished-package storage before goods move to Transportation.

Manages troops, weapons, military equipment, attack and defence compositions, base security, and police-raid response.

Organisation growth

Rank progression expands the organisation from five players to ten at Silver.0, then to a maximum of fifteen at Gold.1. Each milestone unlocks five additional player and department positions and a nearby map expansion. The HQ Boss separately purchases nearby properties to give departments more physical space; property purchases do not unlock player slots.

Departments, logistics, and economy

Departments depend on one another. Production supplies Packaging, Packaging prepares goods for Transportation, and Protection supports security across the organisation. NPC workers follow assigned routes and move physical items through facilities; inventory, shelf capacity, equipment, and available materials affect operations. Normal deliveries are NPC-controlled, while bulk deliveries allow player participation.

RECON uses a physical cash economy with separate HQ Funds and legitimate cash pools, alongside departmental funds. The HQ Boss manages legitimate businesses and money laundering. Departmental expansion, facilities, equipment, troop replacement, repairs, and operations all place demands on the organisation's finances.

Gang warfare and attacks

Any department player can request an attack against a rival organisation, but the HQ Boss must approve it. An attack uses one shared battlefield representing the opposing gang's entire base; departments are not separate attack targets. Players deploy their allocated troops, while NPCs and automated structures defend the target base. Players cannot actively defend against rival-gang attacks, even when online.

  • Attacks run within a one-hour operation window.
  • Stars are awarded for reaching 50% destruction, destroying the HQ, and reaching 100% destruction.
  • Attack outcomes can affect HQ Funds, buildings, defensive structures, troop losses, rank, and respect.
  • Loot percentages, scoring formulas, and several matchmaking edge cases remain provisional or TBA.

Police raids and server event

Police are external NPC opponents. Each gang faces one police raid per week at a random time, with strength scaling by gang rank. Online players can actively defend during a raid; if the gang is offline, NPC troops and automated defences respond. A successful defence protects the stored product load. A failed raid can result in finished goods being seized, while raw materials and items still in production are not seized.

RECON also includes a server-wide Police HQ assault. Police accumulate seized goods across the server; when the shared inventory reaches 80 lb, every gang is notified and has a six-hour opportunity to attack. The first gang to complete the assault claims the reward, currently listed as 75–100 lb. The relationship between the trigger and reward, and whether the event uses a shared or separate HQ instance, remain TBA.

Rank, respect, and progression

Progression is tracked at gang level, not individually. Five leagues—Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, and Ruby—contain four sub-ranks each, for twenty levels in total. Rank unlocks organisation capacity, map areas, department positions, troop types, capacity increases, businesses, equipment availability, and higher cash limits. Respect rises through successful operations and can fall after failures, raids, and defeats; exact scoring and deduction formulas remain TBA.

Design focus

RECON is built around interdependence: every department has a focused responsibility, but the organisation succeeds only when its players coordinate people, physical resources, money, logistics, and security. Its central tension comes from balancing internal operations with rival attacks and unpredictable police events.
