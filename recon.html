PROJECT 06 / GAME CONCEPT + SYSTEMS DESIGN

RECON

A multiplayer strategy and management concept where mafia organisations and police forces recruit, expand, manage operations, and compete for influence.

MULTIPLAYER STRATEGYMANAGEMENTFACTION WARFARE
ORGANISE / OPERATE / OUTPREDICT / OUTRANK

Concept overview

RECON combines organisation management and NPC logistics with base-building, resource progression, and recurring wars. Players establish either a mafia organisation or a police force. Both sides recruit members, manage NPCs, build and upgrade facilities, and compete to expand their influence. Mafia organisations can attack police forces and rival mafias; police organisations can attack mafia groups.

Five positions per organisation

01

Leader

The mafia organisation is led by a Mafia Boss; the police organisation by a Police Chief. Each organisation has four specialised subordinates beneath its leader.

02

Production

Manages NPC workers, production choices, processing stations, and moving finished products into shared storage.

03

Packing

Manages packaging materials, NPC workers, packing stations, and moving finished packages to the designated shelf.

04

Transportation

Plans a single NPC delivery route from its start to destination, chooses transport methods, and monitors the delivery.

05

Protection

Trains soldiers, chooses weapons and equipment, manages troop-capacity limits, and positions defensive structures.

The delivery interception system

This system turns logistics into a prediction problem rather than a direct chase. The mafia transportation role assigns one route to NPC delivery personnel. Police receive information about the product and several possible routes, then choose the route they believe is correct.

  • If police select the actual route, the delivery automatically fails.
  • If police select the wrong route, the delivery succeeds.
  • No extra NPC deployment or resource allocation is required by this interception mechanic.

Economy and weekly wars

Mafia organisations earn money through successful deliveries and other operations, while police receive government funding. Both sides can receive rewards for wars and defeating rivals. Funds are held in a shared organisation account and spent on buildings, equipment, defences, and upgrades. The current concept proposes weekly wars that match organisations of similar rank, with separate police and mafia leaderboards and one attack opportunity per organisation each week — a provisional rule that may change.

Design focus

RECON is a team-based competition in which specialisation matters. Individual players handle focused responsibilities, but their decisions contribute to the strength of a shared organisation. The delivery system adds uncertainty and prediction to the management layer, while recurring wars give long-term progression a clear competitive event.
