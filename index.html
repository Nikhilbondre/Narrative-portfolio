02 / HOW I THINK ABOUT NARRATIVE

The story
belongs in
the system.

A character's behaviour, an environmental clue, or a choice with lasting consequences can carry story without stopping play for an explanation.

01

Character through action

Motivation should be visible in what a character does, how they fight, what they protect, and what they refuse to accept.

↗
02

Worlds that reward attention

Visual details and repeating motifs invite the audience to connect evidence and reach a discovery themselves.

↗
03

Choices that leave a trace

Player-facing narrative can remember a decision and let its emotional meaning return later in a different context.

↗
03 / ABOUT

I'm Nikhil Bondre, an aspiring game writer and narrative designer based in Nagpur, India.

For around two years, I've developed independent story concepts, game worlds, character arcs, boss encounters, and episodic outlines. My work spans dark fantasy, interactive suspense, crime, adventure, and mystery animation. I'm also pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Technology, which makes me curious about how creative ideas connect with systems and implementation.
