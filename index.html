STORY NOTE / 01

Stories don't
just get told.

They leave traces.

A character’s choices should change what comes next. A place should carry the marks of what happened there. And the world should feel like it exists beyond the moment the audience sees it.

That’s what I aim to build across games and animation: stories that unfold through people, places, and consequences.

04 / ABOUT

Writer by instinct.
Designer by curiosity.

I’m an independent writer building toward a career in game writing and narrative design, based in Nagpur, India.

My work moves between interactive game concepts and feature-length animation stories. I have developed dark-fantasy worldbuilding and boss encounters for GENOCIDE, organisation-management and multiplayer systems for RECON, and connected crime stories through The Saxophone Thief and SAIMON. I’m also pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Technology.
