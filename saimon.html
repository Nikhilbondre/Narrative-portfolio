SAIMON

ANIMATED FEATURE / OVERALL STORY OUTLINE

Film overview

SAIMON is a connected animated crime feature told through two interwoven timelines. In the present, government-commissioned journalist Naren interviews Balan, the former cook of the Karriappa family, to document the criminal network they once operated within. Each major answer opens into a flashback, while the present-day investigation continues to uncover new information and concealed loyalties.

The story follows Saimon, the eldest of eight Karriappa siblings. He entered the criminal world to save his family when they faced death over a debt, accepting work under Veriappa — the man who would become known as the original Surgeon. Saimon's violent methods helped the family rise within a much larger criminal network, but the power they accumulated also made them a target.

01 / The family

Balan's account introduces the Karriappa family and their criminal businesses, but also shows the warmth and ordinary intimacy they shared at home. The family is not presented as a collection of isolated villains; their shared meals and relationships establish what is later destroyed. Balan was their cook, a member of the gang, and has always been loyal to Saimon.

02 / The network above them

The film reveals an organisation whose influence stretches across North, South, East, and West. Veriappa is the original Surgeon; Andrew temporarily occupies the role while Veriappa is absent. Madhav, known as the Professor, controls the southern territory and operates through a college that conceals large-scale drug production. The title “Surgeon” carries a terrifying reputation, while the truth about Veriappa's background remains partly uncertain.

03 / The massacre

Andrew orders the Professor to eliminate the Karriappas and recover the hidden cargo. The Professor enters the family mansion and demands the location of their concealed assets. The family refuses to reveal it. The attack leaves the Karriappas dead or missing, and Saimon appears to have been killed. In reality, he survives with help from the family doctor, Ilyas, while the wider network believes him dead or missing.

04 / The cook's real purpose

The interview is more than a retrospective documentary. Balan remains secretly loyal to Saimon and is helping him identify police officers connected to the Professor. When officer Dhanush reacts fearfully to the suggestion that Saimon survived, Saimon becomes suspicious, follows him home, and uses threats against his family to extract information. He ultimately kills Dhanush’s wife and daughter. The present-day investigation changes the meaning of the stories Balan tells about the past: the interview is also helping Saimon identify police links to the Professor.

05 / The car and the hidden cargo

Before the massacre, Sheela concealed the location of the family's weapons, explosives, drugs, gold, and other valuable assets. She marked the location on a car and sent it to America. She was the only person who knew the location. Saimon spends ten years trying to find the vehicle, eventually learning it will be auctioned. He sends Doss, his best friend and trusted subordinate, to retrieve it.

The car is the same vehicle stolen by Rafe in The Saxophone Thief. The garage sequence is revisited from a new perspective, revealing that the white-gloved man is Doss and that his whispered words were “Saimon Karriappa.”

The final reveal

After the interview, Balan's final answer hints that the Karriappas were destroyed by someone with a greater hunger than their own. Saimon puts on his black ski mask, preparing to pursue the hidden assets with Doss. The film then cuts to a final scene: Veriappa, the original Surgeon, is alive. A caller tells him that Saimon has survived. Veriappa says only his name before the film cuts to black.

Connection to The Saxophone Thief
Both films can stand on their own, but the car unites their plots. In the first film, the car is the target of an auction theft and the cause of a garage ambush. In SAIMON, it is revealed to carry the last clue to the hidden cargo Sheela concealed, and the white-gloved figure's identity and whisper are finally explained.

Narrative structure and themes

  • Two timelines: interview and flashbacks gradually change the audience's understanding of the present.
  • Knowledge and fear: Balan has seen Veriappa's face but hides this knowledge because he fears the Surgeon.
  • Family and ambition: the film contrasts the Karriappas' home life with the violence of the network surrounding them.
  • Continuity through objects: the car links the separate film stories without revealing all of its meaning at once.
  • Legacy and unfinished conflict: Veriappa's survival makes clear that the criminal hierarchy extends beyond the family massacre.
