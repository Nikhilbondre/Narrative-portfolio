PROJECT 03 / 1990s ANIMATED CRIME THRILLER

THE SAXOPHONE THIEF

BEGINNING OF SOMETHING BRUTALUNIVERSE TITLE · TO BE ANNOUNCED

A 1990s American crime-noir story about a famous musician whose second life belongs to the criminal underworld. Performance, violence, and police investigation share the same stage.

SEASON 18 EPISODESCRIME / PSYCHOLOGICAL THRILLER / ACTION
AFTER THE LAST NOTE / THE CITY KEEPS ITS SECRETS

Series premise

In 1990s America, a celebrated saxophone player secretly operates as a masked thief. The public sees the musician; the criminal world encounters a dangerous figure whose reasons for killing are intentionally left unresolved. As two experienced detectives investigate the thefts and killings, one of them secretly feeds information to the protagonist. The story builds toward a high-stakes car auction, a betrayal between partners, and a final ambush that leaves the thief without the prize he stole.

The visual direction is retro animated noir: dramatic silhouettes, hard-edged city shapes, deep midnight blues and purples, and selective neon or warm highlights. The page uses that era's mood as inspiration while keeping its own presentation and identity.

The season arc — eight episodes

01

The Performance

At an expensive restaurant, an alarm announces that a rare item has disappeared from a vault during the protagonist's saxophone performance. Guests suspect him, but he leaves safely. Unable to sleep, he returns in disguise, kills those who remain, confirms his identity to the wealthy man who suspected him, and escapes. “Now I can sleep peacefully.”

02

The Investigation

Two detectives with an established working relationship begin investigating the theft and killings. They connect the incidents but do not yet have enough evidence to expose the musician.

03

The Criminal World

The story opens onto gangs, clubs, thefts, and violent confrontations. The protagonist is capable but not invincible, while the detectives continue to follow the trail.

04

Another Side

The protagonist encounters a woman who attracts his attention during one of his criminal activities. She adds a personal dimension but has no major hidden twist planned for this season.

05

Inside Information

The protagonist stays ahead of law enforcement with help from a police insider who shares plans and operations. The audience is not yet told which detective is responsible; even the partners remain unaware.

06

The Auction

A major car-bidding auction is announced. Rival criminal gangs are expected to attend, and security will be heavy. The protagonist targets a car belonging to Mafia A.

07

The Betrayal

The real detective catches the Saxophone Thief. Before the confrontation resolves, the other detective — the hidden insider — raises a gun behind his partner's head. The episode cuts to black before the shot.

08

The Final Score

The finale opens on the cliffhanger: the traitor shoots his partner to protect the protagonist. The thief steals Mafia A's car from the auction, but Mafia B ambushes him at his garage, defeats him, and takes the car. A white-gloved man threatens him into silence. The garage scene ends the anime.

The main players

  • The Saxophone Thief: a celebrated performer living a secret life as a masked thief; his reason for killing remains ambiguous.
  • The detectives: experienced partners who trust their working relationship, unaware that one is secretly helping their target.
  • Mafia A: owns the car targeted at the auction.
  • Mafia B: ambushes the protagonist at the garage and takes the car. Its white-gloved representative remains unidentified.
  • The woman: attracts the protagonist but is not planned as a surprise villain or major twist in Season 1.
The thief gets the car. For a moment. Then the city reminds him who really owns the streets.

Story structure and tone

The season's tension is built through dramatic irony and delayed identification: the audience sees that the protagonist has an unusual double life, while the investigation progresses in parallel. The hidden police insider provides a long-running mystery, and the episode-seven cliffhanger turns the detectives' trust into the season's final betrayal. Some logistics — including how Mafia B locates the garage — are intentionally still open in the outline.
