ANIMATED FEATURE / WORKING OUTLINE

Film overview

The Saxophone Thief is a mature, cinematic animated crime thriller set in 1990s America. Its lead, Rafe (a working name), is a celebrated saxophone player whose public reputation conceals a second life as a masked thief. His reason for killing remains deliberately unexplained at this stage of the story.

The film opens with music before revealing an expensive restaurant where Rafe performs for wealthy guests. A rare item disappears from the restaurant vault during the performance. Suspicion brushes against Rafe, but he leaves without being openly accused. Later, he returns in disguise and the audience sees the violent side of his double life.

01 / The investigation

Two experienced detective partners take on the thefts and killings. They follow evidence through the criminal underworld, but Rafe repeatedly stays one step ahead. Someone inside the police is secretly passing him information. The audience sees the effects of the betrayal before learning which detective is responsible.

02 / The criminal world

The story moves through clubs, thefts, confrontations, and competing criminal organisations. A woman Rafe encounters adds a personal dimension without being turned into a hidden mastermind or an arbitrary twist. The film keeps its attention on Rafe's double identity and the network surrounding him.

03 / The auction and the garage

A high-security car auction draws the major gangs into the same place. Rafe targets and steals a car belonging to one organisation. During his escape, he changes out of his disguise and heads for his garage. A white-gloved figure has been waiting across the street. After a rocket destroys the garage entrance, the man's group takes the car and threatens Rafe, revealing that the theft has served another organisation's interests.

The white-gloved man whispers something to Rafe, but the audience does not hear the words in this film. That withheld identity is the bridge to the next feature.

Story and visual direction

The intended visual approach is mature, cinematic anime: grounded adult character design, controlled facial performances, purposeful compositions, restrained colour design, textured environments, and action with physical weight. The 1990s setting comes through cars, clothing, music, interiors, street life, and period technology rather than a broad retro-cartoon treatment.

  • Dual identity: the contrast between a polished musician and a dangerous thief drives the opening.
  • Delayed reveal: the detective betrayal is held back until the partnership itself is broken.
  • Criminal power shifts: the auction and garage sequence turn an apparent victory into a setup.
  • Shared-world clue: the unseen whisper and the stolen car carry meaning into SAIMON.
Connection to SAIMON
The garage sequence is revisited in SAIMON, where the white-gloved man is identified as Doss. The whisper is revealed to be “Saimon Karriappa.” The car carries the last clue to a hidden cache of assets, making the two films separate stories linked by the same criminal network.

Project status

This is an original animated feature-film outline, not a completed screenplay or produced film. The lead's final name, supporting character names, gang names, and some middle-act details remain open for later development.
